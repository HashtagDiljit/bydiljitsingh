Why project management

The drawing was never the whole problem.

Four years of mechanical engineering was mostly team projects: a rally truck, a hydrogen plant, a two-stroke engine, a bridge we tested to destruction. The technical work was what got marked. What usually decided whether a project actually landed was the coordination around it, and who owed what to whom by when.

That is what moved me toward delivery rather than design. I am on the GROW graduate programme at Arcadis now, supporting project managers on property commissions. It is early. I am still learning how the commercial and contractual side works in practice, and I would rather say that than pretend otherwise.

What I did bring is a habit of checking things properly and writing down what I found, including the parts that did not work. The projects here are the evidence I have so far.
