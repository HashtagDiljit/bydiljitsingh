On method

Precision is a habit, not an event.

Engineering taught me that outcomes are decided long before anything is built. In the tolerances someone chose to accept. In the load case someone chose not to check. The same holds for a programme: the risk that surfaces in month nine was usually visible in month one, to anyone reading carefully.

Progress comes from iteration, not shortcuts. Strong outcomes are built by understanding systems, respecting constraints, and being honest about trade-offs. What lasts is rarely accidental.

I moved from mechanical design into project delivery because the interesting problem was never only the drawing. It was the coordination around it: the sequencing, the dependencies, and the conversations that stop a technical decision from quietly becoming a commercial one.
