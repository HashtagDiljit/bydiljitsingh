Pneumatic Piston Model (IDP1 A)

Parametric modelling and drawing package for a pneumatic piston assembly, focusing on manufacturability and tolerance stack-up.

Role: CAD Lead
Duration: 6 weeks
Tools: SolidWorks
Deliverables: Assembly drawings + BOM
Add piston assembly render here.

Context & Challenge

The brief required a fully defined pneumatic piston model suitable for manufacturing handoff. Critical focus areas were tolerance stack-up, surface finish requirements, and ensuring the assembly could be serviced easily.

Technical Details

Parametric CAD

Created a top-down model with configurable bore, stroke length, and mounting interface to support quick design iterations.

Assembly & Drawings

Produced fully dimensioned drawings, exploded views, and a bill of materials detailing seals, fasteners, and machined components.

Manufacturing Readiness

Defined tolerances and surface finishes for sealing interfaces, verified fitment checks, and documented machining notes for suppliers.

What We Achieved

18 Unique Parts
1 Assembly BOM
100% Drawing Coverage

Delivered a production-ready CAD package with clear tolerances and a clean assembly workflow, enabling smooth handoff to fabrication.
