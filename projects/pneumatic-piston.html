Pneumatic Piston Model (IDP1 A)

Parametric modelling and drawing for a pneumatic piston assembly, focusing on CAD skills and learning.

Role: CAD Designer
Duration: 6 weeks
Tools: Fusion 360
Deliverables: Hand Sketches + Assembly Drawings

Final Pneumatic Piston Model Isometric View.

Context & Challenge

The brief required us to use an image of a pneumatic piston to hand sketch within scale and make a CAD model to produce isometric drawings with a BOM and 2 part drawings.

Technical Details

Hand Sketch

Made a hand sketch at a 2:1 scale, dimensioning all the relevant parts and adding centre and hidden lines.

Assembly & Part Drawings

Final Assembly Drawing with Bill of Materials.

What I Achieved

13 Unique Parts
1 Assembly BOM
100% Drawing Coverage

Delivered a hand sketch with assembly and part drawings with a bill of materials.
