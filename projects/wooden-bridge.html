Wooden Bridge (IDP1 B)

Structural optimisation project focused on maximising load-to-weight performance while meeting strict budget limits.

Role: Designer & Builder
Duration: 8 weeks
Tools: Fusion 360, Practical Assembly
Deliverables: CAD model and hand built model

Dimensioned Bridge Engineering Drawing

Context & Challenge

The brief required a wooden structure capable of withstanding a 1 kN load within a budget of £5.

Objectives & KPIs

Strength

Withstand the 1 kN pass threshold.

Cost

Materials can only be under £5 in total.

Drawing

Produce 1 assembly drawing to help building stage.
