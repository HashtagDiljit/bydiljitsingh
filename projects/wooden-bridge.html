Wooden Bridge (IDP1 B)

Structural optimisation project focused on maximising load-to-weight performance while meeting strict deflection limits.

Role: Structural Analyst
Duration: 8 weeks
Tools: ANSYS, SolidWorks
Outcome: Highest strength-to-weight in cohort
Add bridge prototype photography here.

Context & Challenge

The brief required a lightweight wooden structure capable of withstanding a high compressive load with limited material. We balanced manufacturability, geometry, and adhesive constraints while targeting the highest load-to-weight ratio.

Objectives & KPIs

Strength

Withstood 2.4 kN vs 1.1 kN pass threshold.

Weight

Optimised truss geometry with minimal mass.

Deflection

Stayed within allowable mid-span deflection.

Technical Details

Finite Element Analysis

Simulated stress distribution and failure modes under incremental loading to guide brace placement and joint reinforcement.

Manufacturing Approach

Standardised cut lengths, adhesive curing sequence, and jig alignment to maintain dimensional accuracy throughout assembly.

What We Achieved

2.4 kN Load Capacity
#1 Load-to-Weight
0 mm Bond Failures

Delivered the strongest bridge in the cohort with a clean documentation trail and clear structural testing results.
